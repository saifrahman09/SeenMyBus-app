Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026
Privacy-First Architecture

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

At SeenMyBus, we believe in radical data minimalism. The platform is designed exclusively to serve the campus student body without profiling users, harvesting personal identities, or monetizing telemetry.

2. Information We NEVER Collect

To ensure total peace of mind for every commuter, the platform strictly avoids collecting:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or student ID numbers are required to browse or submit spots.
  • No User Account Credentials: There are no account creations, passwords, or social logins.
  • No Continuous Real-Time GPS Tracking: The application does not monitor your live physical movement or trace device routes.

3. Information We Process (Anonymous Telemetry)

To enable crowdsourced updates and prevent vandalism, the platform processes the following minimal, pseudonymous metadata:

  • Hardware Hash Fingerprint: A deterministic, one-way hash derived locally from non-identifying screen dimensions, canvas rendering metrics, and browser headers. This allows the system to credit your Monthly Contributor Rank across cache resets without knowing your identity.
  • Crowdsourced Submissions: Publicly submitted bus parking bay choices and departure triggers stored in our real-time database.
  • Local Client State: Browser `localStorage` variables used solely to store preferences (such as notification opt-in states and interactive tutorial dismissals).

4. Automated Data Deletion & Storage Lifecycle

We keep cloud database memory clean through strictly scheduled deletion lifecycles:

  • Shift Expiry Purging: Stale bus parking locations are automatically dropped after 90 minutes of inactivity or immediately following daily shift departure cutoffs.
  • Monthly Leaderboard Cycling: All contributor scores are partitioned by calendar cycle and reset on the 5th of every month, clearing out legacy user profile allocations.

5. Browser Notifications

Push notifications (for route modifications or unassigned slot reminders) are entirely client-managed. Permissions can be granted or revoked at any point directly within your browser settings. We never use notification channels for marketing, advertising, or unrelated announcements.

6. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

The Application relies on trusted cloud infrastructure providers to operate:

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database: Secure real-time synchronization of anonymous bus slot records.
  • Netlify: Global edge delivery of static web assets and client-side code.
