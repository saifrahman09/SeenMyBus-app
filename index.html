Hey, a little note for you

SeenMyBus doesn't use GPS or receive official live updates from the college. It relies mainly on student-shared information, so things can sometimes be inaccurate or delayed. But that's also what makes this our app. I built it as a student, for students. If you spot your bus, know a location, or have an update — add it! Your little contribution can help someone else catch their bus. Use it, help it grow, and please tell me when something's wrong. Every bit of feedback helps.

Welcome to SeenMyBus

It all started with one question we AJU students face every single day after classes:
"Has anybody seen my bus?"

We all know the daily chaos—running around campus, calling friends, rushing to grab a seat, and guessing which bus number will actually be assigned to our route today.

An App powered
by Students

Just like tracking a live train, SeenMyBus gives you the real-time info. of every bus parked on campus. Good part? No GPS or location permissions required, it's community driven—just students helping students.

See it in action

1. Explore the Map

Pinch to zoom! We've added a dummy bus to your map. Tap the glowing yellow dot on the map above to view its details.

2. Keep Map Accurate

Tap the "Yes" or "No" button above to confirm if it's correct or incorrect. This community check filters out wrong updates!

3. Search Routes

Tap the search bar at the top of the screen to quickly filter the list. Try typing a route or bus no., then click Next!

4. Fast Navigation

Tap any bus in the route list below the map to instantly fly to its exact parking location!

5. Switch to Digital Board

Tap the Digital Board tab to see a real-time list of all routes and their assigned buses.

6. Edit Bus Numbers

Tap the Edit icon button to quickly assign or update buses straight from the board view!

7. Instant Updates

Type the new bus number below. It saves to the board instantly without jumping to the map!

You're All Set!

Add, update, or fetch buses seamlessly to keep the AJU community informed.
