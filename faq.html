Troubleshooting & Quick Fixes

Map or Slots Not Loading?

Tap your browser's Refresh / Reload button to re-establish the live real-time WebSocket connection.

App Freezing or Updates Not Showing?

Your browser may be holding older static scripts. Clear your Browser Cache / Site Data (or open in an Incognito tab) so your device pulls the latest version.

How to Use SeenMyBus

Simple 3-second workflows to keep everyone updated:

1
Find Your Bus

Search your route name (e.g., "Adityapur") or bus number in the top search bar, or tap directly on the map circle to focus on the parking bay.

2
Park / Assign an Arrived Bus (+10 pts)

Tap Update Bus Status > Bus Parked > Select Route > Pick Bus Number > Tap the bay on the map to confirm.

3
Mark a Departed Bus (+5 pts)

When a bus leaves campus, tap Update Bus Status > Bus Departed > Tap "Mark Departed" next to that bus.

Frequently Asked Questions

SeenMyBus uses crowdsourced consensus. When students arrive at the transport yard or board their bus, they confirm the parking slot number. When multiple independent spotters select the same slot, the system locks it in as verified campus consensus.

A single conflicting input cannot override a location confirmed by multiple students. The database prioritizes consensus reports and high-reputation contributors.
